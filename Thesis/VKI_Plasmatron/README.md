# Aerothermodynamics of the damaged surface · von Karman Institute

**In progress.** Results will be published here after the thesis.

The second phase of the thesis asks how impact damage changes the heat load on a thermal protection surface.
The reference experiment is the **VKI Plasmatron** in its **supersonic flat-plate configuration**: a high-enthalpy flow over a
flat sample, with **shock waves**, a supersonic boundary layer and, on damaged samples, separation and reattachment
inside the cavity.

Planned work, with a high-order discontinuous Galerkin (DG) solver:

1. Reproduce the flow of the facility and validate it against published Plasmatron data.
2. Pristine flat plate: wall heat flux and shear stress.
3. Cavities representative of impact damage: local heating, hot spots at the edges.
4. **Coupled flow–thermal** response of the sample, compared with Plasmatron measurements.
